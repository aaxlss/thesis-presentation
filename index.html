The Challenge in B2B Sales

  • Sales departments waste effort on low-conversion prospects.
  • Goal: Transform static pipelines into dynamic, probability-based early warning systems.
  • Requires interpretable models for real-world sales strategy.

Phase 2 & 3: Partitioning

  • 80/20 Stratified Train-Test Split.
  • Training Data (80%): Used for model learning and balancing.
  • Testing Data (20%): Completely untouched, preserves native "Won"/"Loss" distribution for honest evaluation.

Phase 2 & 3: Data Balancing

Applied strictly to Training Data.

  • SMOTE: Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique via nearest-neighbor interpolation.
  • ADASYN: Adaptive Synthetic sampling focusing on hard-to-learn minority instances.

Phase 4: AI Modeling & Tuning

  • Pipeline developed in Python 3 (scikit-learn, XGBoost, PyTorch).
  • Hyperparameter tuning optimized for F1 Score to balance precision and recall.
  • Overcoming the risk of models simply guessing "Loss" every time.

Phase 4: Architectures Compared

Traditional Machine Learning

  • Random Forest: Bagging ensemble, stable baseline.
  • XGBoost: Gradient-boosting framework. The benchmark standard.

Modern Deep Learning

  • FT-Transformer: Feature tokenization with multi-head self-attention.
  • TabNet: Sequential sparse attention mechanism.

Phase 5: Evaluation Criteria

  • Accuracy: Percentage of overall correct predictions (can be misleading).
  • F1 Score: Balance of Precision (correct wins) and Recall (catching all possible wins).
  • ROC-AUC: Threshold-independent discriminative ability (1.0 is perfect).

The "Deep Learning Gap"

Result: Traditional tree-based models decisively outclassed Modern Deep Learning.

  • XGBoost proved highly precise in distinguishing success vs. failure.
  • Deep Learning models plateaued (~86.8% accuracy) and struggled severely with full feature sets.
  • Data Efficiency: Top models maintained accuracy at 75% features but collapsed at 25%.

Actionable Sales Insights

What drives a successful B2B close?

  • Client History: Returning clients exhibit win rates exceeding 82%.
  • Channel Strategy: Resellers (27.6% win rate) outperform Field Sales (18.6%).
  • Deal Velocity: Successful deals have steady movement; lost deals stagnate.
  • Opportunity Health: High-dollar proposals face a much higher rejection rate.

Accuracy vs. GDPR Compliance

The Ethical Accuracy-Calibration Paradox

  • XGBoost: Most accurate overall.
  • Random Forest: Yielded a vastly lower Expected Calibration Error (ECE - 0.0326 vs 0.0674).
  • Takeaway: Random Forest provides more "trustworthy" probability scores, aligning better with GDPR's "Right to an Explanation".

Conclusion & Future Work

  • Gradient Boosting (XGBoost) remains the apex for tabular B2B forecasting.
  • Transparency and Model Actionability dictate practical success.
  • Future Work: Incorporating unstructured data (emails/transcripts) via NLP and expanding hardware scaling.
